About Claw Glancer

An asymmetric, pre-disclosure threat intelligence platform designed to bypass the information degradation curve.

The Asymmetric Advantage

By the time vulnerability reporting reaches mainstream commercial news and polished blogs, its strategic defensive value has degraded significantly. Adversaries automate reconnaissance against edge infrastructure within minutes of public exploit release.

Claw Glancer operates on an upstream intelligence paradigm: intercepting raw maintainer advisories (Openwall oss-security), tracking confirmed weaponized exploits (Nuclei, Metasploit, PacketStorm), and extracting pre-auth remote attack vectors directly from telemetry.

Core Intelligence Pipelines

  • Pre-Disclosure Vanguard: Ingests the Openwall oss-security plain-text feed to capture embargo expirations and zero-days days before NVD indexes them.
  • Weaponization & Exploitation Reality: Correlates against CISA KEV (with active Ransomware Campaign tracking), EPSS exploit probability percentiles, and public exploit repositories.
  • Adversarial AI & MLOps Coverage: Specialized kill-chain tracking for prompt injection, context window manipulation, unsafe deserialization (e.g. torch.load), and agent execution bypasses.
  • Actionable Analyst Arsenal: Instant Shodan attack surface pivots and one-click Markdown incident briefs for incident response teams.
  • Zero-Server Architecture: Runs as a high-performance static client powered by automated pipeline workflows.

Design Philosophy

  • Speed: Instant client-side filtering by tactical presets (War Room, Pre-Auth, AI Infra, Ransomware).
  • Explainability: Hover over any threat score to see its exact mathematical breakdown.
  • Hostile Brevity: Technical, unambiguous indicators replacing corporate PR summaries.
